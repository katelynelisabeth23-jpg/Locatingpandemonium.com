Let’s make it happen - I’d love to hop on a quick 10-15 minute phone call with you to set up a game plan. Do you have time for a chat around 5:30 today, or would you like to schedule something for a time that works better for you?

Yeah 5:30 works for me

  1. A lead comes in from RR.
  2. My saved intro texts go out, sent by me or by Claude when I say so. SMS clients get theirs by hand. Claude can always read the client’s texts, and drafts the reply.
  3. Once a call time is confirmed, Claude adds it to my calendar.
  4. After the disco call I send a recap text, then Lena starts the search. That’s Marisa’s list, next.

Why they work: each text asks one easy question, so a new lead can reply in a second, and the third one turns that reply into a booked call. Marisa went from first text to a call time in minutes.

Move-in date: from my CRM notes first, then the client’s latest texts, and if it’s still unknown, Claude asks me.

What this saves me: no retyping the same intro texts, and no back-and-forth to get the call on my calendar.

Quiet leads: a short follow-up run, 1 to 2 days apart, then a call or a voicemail.
